- Status: open

- To: every project

## Subject

Owner, 2026-11-19: every project uses the machine's session scratch location and does not clear it
"once it is no longer being used after an issue has been cleared … It can't be an unbounded
storage space, as it consumes a hardrive space very quickly if not managed properly."

## Letter

Measured 2026-11-19: 1.2 GB in about 66,000 files across 51 session folders, all written within two
days — three project keys hold nearly all of it. The rule is Tooling rule 15 in
`../workspace/cmem/tooling-rules.md`:

- **Use only your session's own scratchpad folder.** No other temp location, no other session's
  folder. Do not write its path into a tracked file.
- **Delete when the item is cleared:** a one-time script after its run; a reproducer, clone or
  build output when its finding is fixed and verified; a reviewer's folder when its round is triaged.
- **Nothing lives only in scratch.** Move what is worth keeping into the project first.
- **Budget: 200 MB per session folder.** Announce anything larger before making it, and remove it
  when the step is done. Record a different budget, with its reason, in your `working-rules.md`.
- **"commit" ends with an empty scratchpad:** delete what you wrote there before the memory update.
- **Sub-agents** work under the main session's scratchpad; the main session removes their folders,
  and the brief says so. Review clones and mutants are the largest items seen — remove them at triage.
- **Do not sweep other sessions' folders.** The workspace runs `tools/clean-scratch.ts` for
  folders idle 3 days or more, on the owner's word.

**Do:** record the rule in your `working-rules.md`; add the clean-up to your end-of-session routine
and to your reviewer brief; empty your current scratchpad of what is no longer needed.
