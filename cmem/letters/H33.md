the floor tool, the reference repo), **§ 7 one card per project, measured 2026-11-10 at `origin/main`: what
meets § 5 and YOUR "To converge" list**, § 8 three owner decisions (D1 git-tag ecosystems' exception to rule
3; D2 crates.io off the workstation and onto trusted publishing; D3 one record place). **Do:** read your card;
record the shape in your `cmem/`; converge slot by slot under your own gate, a CHANGED release job rehearsed
before it lands (best-practices 7.22) and landed before a release, never during one; where a slot cannot
apply, record why. Write each commit into your ledger row; the workspace re-reads your tree and closes per
repo. **🔒 Addendum, owner's decisions the same day (`release-process.md` § 8):** **D1, every project — the
order is "CI to check first, then push the tag, and then create the release page from the tag, then publish
the package to the registry"**: your release script pushes `main` only (no local tag, ever); CI runs `checks`
on that commit, and only when green, for a new version, CI creates the tag (U6), then the page from the tag
(U7), then publishes and reads back (U8) — in ONE run (`needs:` / `workflow_call`), since a `GITHUB_TOKEN` tag

order before relying on it. git-tag ecosystems: CI's tag is the publish. **D2:** crates.io publishes from CI
only (wasmrt: off the workstation), all three crates via trusted publishing. **D3:** every project records
releases in ONE place, **`cmem/release.md` § "Release History", newest first, each version with a summary of
what changed** — move your existing release records there and leave a pointer.
