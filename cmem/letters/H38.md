## Letter

🔒 **Owner, 2026-11-17: "Project memory guide (cmem)".** `../workspace/cmem/memory-guide.md` is the owner's document in
its words; read it — this is the summary. **Why:** a session should learn where the project stands from one
small file and open anything else only when its task needs it (measured 2026-11-17: four projects hold 1.2–1.8
MB of notes; single files of 356–384 KB; wasmtk's INDEX 34 KB; about 2,600 lines over 200 characters).
**Layout:** root `CLAUDE.md` (untracked) points at `cmem/STATE.md` and `cmem/INDEX.md` and holds nothing else;
`STATE.md` — where the project stands now (template in the guide; headings `## Now`, `## Review`, `## Next`,
`## Waiting on owner` required; rewritten, never appended; counts only, no issue content, no machine facts);
`INDEX.md` — a router, one line per file saying WHEN to read it, each under 120 characters, no rules or
history; `working-rules.md` (the rules, present tense); `open-work.md` (OPEN items only);
`design-decisions.md` (decisions and how a rule changed); `review-process.md`; topic files, one subject each
(`platforms.md`, `release.md`, testing, architecture …); `review-issues.md` (gitignored); **`cmem/archive/`**
— closed items and superseded notes, grouped by release, IDs kept, read by nobody unless asked; `cmem/local/`.
Working artifacts (probe outputs, review mutants) leave `cmem/` for a folder of their own. **Limits, held by
the size check:** INDEX.md 3 KB; STATE.md 60 lines; any other live note 25 KB; any line outside a code block
200 characters; `archive/` and `local/` unlimited; a project may record a different value with its reason in
`working-rules.md`. **Writing rules:** one subject per file with a summary and a last-verified date at the
top; one fact per line; a stable ID on every tracked item; the rule, not its history; open and closed never
share a file; link, do not restate. **Session routine:** start — STATE.md, INDEX.md, your workspace
correspondence rows, then only the topic files STATE's "Read next" or the task names; end — rewrite STATE.md,
move closed items to `archive/`, record decisions and rules, run the size check and fix what it reports. A
reviewer gets its brief and STATE.md and does not browse `cmem`; any other sub-agent gets the exact files the
main agent picked from the index. **Migrate, in this order** (steps 1–3 give most of the saving, one session):
(1) write STATE.md; (2) cut INDEX.md to a router — its rules to `working-rules.md`, its history to
`design-decisions.md`; (3) point `CLAUDE.md` at the two files; (4) move closed items to `cmem/archive/` (open
review issues go to `review-issues.md`, not the archive); (5) split files still over 25 KB; (6) wrap or split
lines over 200 characters; (7) copy `../workspace/tools/check-cmem.ts` to `scripts/check-cmem.ts` (Bun; no dependencies;
`--summary` for counts) and run it at the end of every session. **Where you stand** (the owner's table, in the
guide; re-run it on your own tree): every repo lacks STATE.md and has an INDEX over 3 KB; files over 25 KB /
lines over 200 — wasmtk 16 / 721, binaryang 19 / 582, wazmrt 11 / 362, wasmrt 9 / 278, zilc 8 / 211, S3D 5 /
143, zailc 2 / 126, the loaders and rsxtk 0–1 / 3–51 (those can pass in one session). Where two projects hold
the same file (`interop.md` in wazmrt and wasmrt) both copies stay; name one owner. **Held for the owner's
answer:** your ★ best-practices copy (leave it as it is until decided — do not delete or split it). Answer
with your commit per migration step and your check's summary line; the workspace re-runs the check on your
`origin/main` and closes per repo.
